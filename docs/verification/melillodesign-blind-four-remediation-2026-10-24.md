# Four blind walkthroughs: findings, repairs and fresh acceptance

The purpose is to repair the document journey before testing it with a new reader. Passing means that an unfamiliar engineer can complete the journey through the controls and their visible state. Additional explanatory paragraphs do not count as a repair.

## Evidence and method

No source, product specification, expected answers, other review or prior project conversation was supplied. Raw prompts, responses, timestamps and full transcriptions are under `.tmp/verification/blind-four-2026-10-24/`; `reviewer-1-report.md` through `reviewer-4-report.md` preserve their answers.

The fresh creation journey reached header/footer publication but stalled before body proposals. A separate, explicitly identified existing project was then used to inspect comparison, preview and editing. It is not evidence that fresh creation succeeded.

Round 25's accessibility transcription omitted visible body-block labels. Round 26 corrected this with all rendered text and measured visible block rectangles. All four then distinguished the layouts. The earlier doubt is a transcription limitation, not a confirmed interface defect. A reviewer preferred a different proposal from the majority; the facilitator's choice is not a selection bug. Text-only readers cannot approve visual quality; the facilitator's visual approval is identified separately.
