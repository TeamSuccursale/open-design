## Product contract at this checkpoint

MelilloDesign supports the engineer's graphic work. The engineer supplies and owns the technical content and decisions. The app reads project context, prepares a searchable asset gallery, helps establish document structure, and develops the selected composition into a designed document.

Projects contain named documents. Normal creation collects identity, document type and folders; the remaining preparation belongs in chat. Fast creation offers a shorter start while retaining document identity and the relevant editorial choices. Related settings belong in grouped questions rather than a long series of isolated turns.

A page-body Blockout is a schematic arrangement of functional blocks. It does not require the project's actual photographs, drawings or finished text to be inserted. The user may attach faint gallery references to express an intention. Approved headers and footers can appear around that schematic body because their recurring graphics are settled earlier. The editor remains usable after design, with an optional faint guide of the finished page and a structured request to adapt its composition. Watermarks and other page-wide decoration need not be confined to content blocks.

Choosing a proposal, editing its saved structure, sending instructions to the agent, and approving the transition to design are distinct actions. Their clarity must be tested through real interactions. CLI execution permissions are also distinct from the user's creative approval of a design; changing one must not silently change the other.
